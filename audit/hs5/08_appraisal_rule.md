# Quality appraisal rule (check A8), written 2026-11-07 before tiers were assigned

This is a first, light appraisal to weight sources in the synthesis. It is not AMSTAR 2; AMSTAR 2 is still planned for the review-type sources (Assignment 09, Section 8).

- **Tier 1 (higher weight):** peer reviewed AND reports a stated method suited to its claim: a systematic review with a reported search protocol, an experiment or benchmark, a design-science artefact with an evaluation, a structured multi-method study, or a formal (for example legal) analysis.
- **Tier 2 (medium weight):** peer reviewed (or an edited book chapter) but descriptive, conceptual, or a non-systematic review; or a framework proposal without an evaluation.
- **Tier 3 (low weight):** not peer reviewed (preprint, repository article, policy note, opinion piece, conference abstract, whole book with unclear review), peer review unclear, or peer reviewed with serious quality flags recorded in the tracker.

Evidence used: the tracker's Source Type and Reason columns, the public abstract where available, and the provenance check (04_provenance_openalex.csv). Full texts were not read for this appraisal.
